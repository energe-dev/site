Why choose an on-grid system?

Maximum savings with
the lowest complexity

On-grid systems connect directly to the electrical grid, allowing you to consume solar energy when the sun shines and draw from the grid when needed. No batteries, no complexity — the most cost-effective way to reduce energy costs for industries and businesses.

Up to 80% reduction in your electricity bill
We size the system to your actual consumption profile to maximize savings from the very first month of operation.
Inject surplus energy into the grid
Under Law 27.424 you can inject energy you don't consume and receive credit on your electricity bill through bidirectional net metering.
3–5 year payback period
Payback depends on your consumption, tariff, and local solar irradiation. Our engineers calculate the exact ROI for your project.
In-house engineering
We design, install, and certify every system through our own engineering team — no intermediaries, full accountability.
Sustainability and ESG goals
Reduce your carbon footprint and meet corporate environmental commitments without disrupting operations.

How we work

From consultation
to live operation

01
Free energy assessment
We analyze your electricity bills, consumption profile, and site conditions. We calculate the optimal system capacity and project the real savings with a detailed ROI breakdown.
02
Engineering and executive project
Our engineering team designs the complete system: panel sizing, inverters, wiring, structure, and protections. Full compliance with applicable regulations (AEA 90364, IRAM, Law 27.424).
03
Installation and commissioning
We manage civil works, structure assembly, panel and inverter installation, and grid connection. Our own crews, on-time delivery.
04
Certification and utility connection
We handle all technical documentation for bidirectional meter approval with your local utility, enabling net metering under Law 27.424.
05
Monitoring and post-installation support
Access a real-time monitoring portal. Our team tracks system performance and provides technical support throughout the entire lifespan of the project.
