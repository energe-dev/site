Why our system?

Engineered for
extreme conditions

01
Total autonomy without grid connection
The system operates in island mode: it generates, stores, and distributes energy completely autonomously, with zero dependency on the local utility. Ideal for locations without grid access or with unreliable supply.
02
High-density LiFePO4 storage
Lithium iron phosphate battery technology delivers the optimal combination of energy density, safety, and longevity. With over 8,000 cycles at 80% depth of discharge, the system exceeds 20 years of useful life.
03
Climatized shelter for extreme climates
The integrated enclosure features high-performance sandwich insulation, industrial air conditioning, and backup heating. It keeps all components within optimal operating temperature from −20 °C to +50 °C.
04
Rapid deployment, no civil works
The system arrives pre-assembled and pre-wired. Commissioning time is hours, not weeks. It ships on a standard truck and can be relocated as operational needs change.
05
Built-in safety and remote monitoring
Per-module aerosol fire suppression, temperature alarms, advanced BMS, and real-time remote monitoring. Generation data, state of charge, and alerts accessible from any device, anywhere.
