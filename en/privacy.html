Privacy
Policy

Last updated: April 2026  ·  Applicable in Argentina

This Privacy Policy governs the processing of personal data collected through the website energe.com.ar and its forms, in compliance with Law No. 25.326 on Personal Data Protection of the Argentine Republic and its regulatory provisions.

1. Data Controller

The data controller for personal data is Energe, with registered address at Alsina 2550, Maipú, Mendoza (PC M5511), Argentina. You may contact us for any query related to your data at: contacto@energe.com.ar or by phone at +54 261 242 4493.

2. Data we collect

We collect only the data you voluntarily provide through our forms:

  • Contact form: full name, email address, phone number, province and message.
  • Solar quote tool: installation type, estimated consumption, province, name, company, email address, phone number and additional comments.

We do not collect sensitive data (as defined by Art. 2 of Law 25.326), nor data from individuals under 18 years of age. This site does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics tools.

3. Purpose of processing

The data collected is used exclusively to:

  • Respond to commercial and technical enquiries related to our photovoltaic services.
  • Prepare personalised budgets and energy diagnostics.
  • Contact you to follow up on your enquiry or quotation.
  • Send Energe commercial information when you have expressly requested to receive it.

We do not use your data for purposes other than those indicated, nor do we share it with third parties without your explicit consent, except as required by law.

4. Legal basis for processing

The processing of your data is based on consent freely given when completing and submitting our forms. In no case is the provision of data mandatory, although its omission may prevent us from responding to your enquiry.

5. Data retention

We retain your data for the time necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was collected and, in any case, during the legally applicable statute of limitations for commercial relationships in Argentina. Once that period expires, data is securely deleted.

6. Information security

We adopt reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss, alteration or improper disclosure, in compliance with Art. 9 of Law 25.326 and Provision DNPDP No. 11/2006.

7. Your rights

In accordance with Art. 14 and related articles of Law 25.326, you have the right to:

  • Access: know what data we hold about you and how we process it.
  • Rectification: correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: request the removal of your data when it is no longer necessary or you have withdrawn consent.
  • Objection: object to the processing of your data for direct marketing purposes.

To exercise any of these rights, send an email to contacto@energe.com.ar with the subject "Data Rights" stating your full name and the right you wish to exercise. We respond within a maximum of 5 business days.

Supervisory authority: If you consider that the processing of your data violates applicable regulations, you may lodge a complaint with the Agencia de Acceso a la Información Pública (AAIP), the enforcement authority of Law 25.326, at www.argentina.gob.ar/aaip.

8. International data transfers

Data collected through our forms is processed and stored within the Argentine Republic. Should any technology service provider we use process data outside the country, we ensure that adequate safeguards are in place in accordance with current regulations.

9. Changes to this policy

Energe reserves the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Amendments will be published on this same page with an indication of the update date. We recommend reviewing it periodically.
